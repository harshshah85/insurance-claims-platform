Architecture Diagrams — L1 & L2

Life & DI Claims Platform — System Context, Containers & Phased Strategy

Prepared by Harsh Shah  |  June 2026

C4 model, two levels. L1 (System Context) shows the platform as one box surrounded by the people and external systems it talks to. L2 (Container) opens the box into its runtime pieces — the Kafka backbone, the 9 bounded-context services, the read models, and the serving APIs. Lower levels (component / code) are intentionally out of scope for a reference architecture.

3. Phased Strategy — 3-Year Roadmap

Deliver a measurable signal early; defer ML until there is clean labeled history.

Year 1 — Backbone & First Signal

Stand up the Kafka backbone, Schema Registry, and the event-sourced Claim aggregate. Ship FNOL intake across the primary channels and the Life death-claim happy path with rule-based STP. Policy Admin CDC live. Read models for client/advisor status. Value: faster acknowledgement and visible status — measurable cycle-time improvement before any ML spend.

Year 2 — Full Domain & Money Movement

Add DI claims (recurring benefit, recertification, offsets), the full money-movement saga, omnichannel correspondence with preference/consent, and rule-based fraud scoring + SIU routing. All 9 bounded contexts in production. Value: rising STP rate, controlled disbursement, fraud leakage reduction.

Year 3 — Intelligence & Decommission

Introduce ML propensity for STP soft-factors and a supervised fraud model trained on 18+ months of labeled SIU outcomes (hard guardrails stay as rules). Predictive DI recertification. Self-service FNOL. Begin legacy claims-system decommission. Value: higher automation, lower run cost.

Why this sequencing

PrincipleRationale
Event backbone before featuresEvery later capability is just another consumer of the log — the backbone is the leverage point
Life before DILife is terminal and simpler; it proves the platform before the durative DI complexity (recertification, offsets) is added
Rules before MLExplainable from day one for regulators; ML needs labeled history that doesn't exist at launch
Measurable Year-1 ROICycle-time and acknowledgement improvements sustain executive sponsorship through the larger Year-3 investment
