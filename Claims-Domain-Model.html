Claims Domain Model

Life & DI Claims Platform — Bounded Contexts, Aggregates & Lifecycle

Prepared by Harsh Shah  |  June 2026

Modeling approach: Domain-Driven Design over an event-sourced core. The model is defined as 9 bounded contexts, 11 aggregates, and a 36-event domain catalog (defined in the Event Streaming Architecture doc). Life (death/mortality) and DI (income replacement) share one platform but have distinct lifecycle state machines, modeled explicitly below.

1. Bounded Contexts

Nine contexts, each owning its events, its aggregates, and its team. Contexts integrate only through published events — never shared tables.

1 · Intake & FNOL

Captures First Notice of Loss from every channel (client portal, advisor desktop, service-rep phone, secure message, mail/upload, IVR). Owns the channel-agnostic intake contract and the initial claim creation.

2 · Policy & Coverage

Resolves the claim to a policy via Policy Admin CDC. Confirms in-force status, coverage type, face amount / benefit amount, and contestability window. Quarantines unresolved policy numbers.

3 · Party & Identity

Models claimant, insured (decedent for Life), beneficiary, advisor, and servicing rep. Owns identity verification (IDV/KYC) and beneficiary designation resolution.

4 · Requirements

Tracks outstanding requirements (death certificate, proof of loss, attending-physician statement, claimant statement, beneficiary forms). Raises, satisfies, waives, and ages requirements.

5 · Adjudication & Decisioning

Computes the STP eligibility score, routes to the low-touch or manual path, and records the approve / deny / pend decision with rationale and the responsible authority.

6 · Fraud & SIU

Scores fraud risk, refers high-risk claims to the Special Investigations Unit, and tracks investigation outcome (cleared / substantiated). Blocks the low-touch path on referral.

7 · Disability Management

DI-only. Manages recurring benefit periods, periodic recertification, return-to-work, benefit offsets (e.g. Social Security, other coverage), and benefit termination.

8 · Payments & Money Movement

Owns the disbursement saga: schedule, hold, dual-control approval, OFAC, tax withholding, settlement, and reconciliation. (Detailed in Correspondence & Money Movement.)

9 · Correspondence & Compliance

Drives inbound/outbound correspondence and enforces regulatory timers (acknowledge, decide, pay) and the immutable audit record. (Detailed in its own docs.)

3. Claim Lifecycle — Life (Death/Mortality)

A Life claim is terminal: it pays a face amount once and closes. Contestability is the defining branch.

NOTIFIED └─► INTAKE (FnolCaptured, PolicyResolved | PolicyUnresolved→QUARANTINE) └─► DOCUMENTS_PENDING (RequirementRaised: death certificate, proof, beneficiary forms) └─► IN_REVIEW (requirements satisfied, StpEligibilityScored, FraudScored) ├─► CONTESTABILITY_REVIEW (if within 2-yr window → manual underwriting review) ├─► SIU_HOLD (if fraud risk ≥ 60) └─► DECISION ├─► APPROVED ─► PAYMENT_SCHEDULED ─► PAID ─► CLOSED ├─► DENIED ─► CLOSED (reason code + provision cited) └─► PENDED ─► back to DOCUMENTS_PENDING / IN_REVIEW side states: WITHDRAWN, REOPENED (e.g. additional beneficiary), QUARANTINE
Contestability is the Life-specific gate: within the 2-year contestability window, an approval cannot be straight-through — the claim is forced into CONTESTABILITY_REVIEW regardless of STP score, because the insurer may investigate material misrepresentation on the application. The STP path resumes only for non-contestable claims.

4. Claim Lifecycle — Disability Income (DI)

A DI claim is durative: after approval it pays a recurring benefit and must be periodically recertified until return-to-work, benefit exhaustion, or termination.

NOTIFIED ─► INTAKE ─► ELIGIBILITY_REVIEW (elimination period, occupation definition, APS) └─► DECISION ├─► APPROVED ─► BENEFIT_ACTIVE (BenefitPeriodOpened; recurring disbursement) │ │ │ ├─◄─► RECERTIFICATION (RecertificationDue → Received; loops each period) │ ├───► OFFSET_APPLIED (SSDI / other coverage reduces benefit) │ ├───► SUSPENDED (recert overdue / RTW pending verification) │ └───► RETURN_TO_WORK | BENEFIT_EXHAUSTED ─► CLOSED └─► DENIED ─► CLOSED
Why DI gets its own context (Disability Management): Life closes after one payment; DI runs for months or years with recurring money movement and recurring regulatory recertification. The RecertificationDue timer (a punctuated Kafka Streams wall-clock timer, see Event Streaming Architecture §6) is the heartbeat of an active DI claim.
